Our Crazy Origin Story

Once upon a time, a bunch of brilliant minds got together and thought, 'Hey, kids are awesome, and learning should be too!' So, they decided to build a place where young brains could go wild with curiosity and creativity. No boring lectures here, just pure, unadulterated fun with learning.

We started this whole shebang in a garage, fueled by lukewarm coffee and a shared dream. Our initial goal was simple: make learning so exciting that kids would forget they were even doing it. Turns out, that's a lot harder than it sounds, but we're getting there!

From those humble beginnings, Brain For Kids has grown into a vibrant community of educators, innovators, and super-smart kids. We're still the same passionate bunch, just with better coffee and a lot more space to make magic happen.

To be the spark that ignites a lifelong love for learning in every child.
